Notifications

Emit Behaviour

The error toast stays until it is dismissed; the others expire on the schedule in src/durations.ts. A failure that vanishes after four seconds is worse than no toast at all — the user is left believing the save succeeded.

Open the console: every removal, whether clicked or expired, is republished on the bus as NOTIFICATION_DISMISSED. Nothing subscribes to it yet, and it is the half of the contract that lets an emitter correlate a toast it owns.

There is no mount point in this page. The region is position: fixed, which is why the shell can mount this micro-frontend anywhere in the document.
